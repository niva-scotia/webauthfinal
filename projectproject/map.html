About This Map

A hand-drawn guide to the best climbing spots — from gym bouldering to outdoor multi-pitch. Each location is tagged by discipline and difficulty range.

Indoor Boulder
Method Climbing Gym

My home gym, perfect for indoor bouldering focused climbing. Has super fun and creative route setting, a great community, and a wide range of V0–V10 problems in different styles. So, whether you're an overhang warrior or a slab enthusist, everyone will find the kind of climbs they vibe with at all skill levels. The setting is also changed frequently enough so that there's always something new, but most problems stay up long enough to project. It's a great balance between projecting and trying new things

Indoor Top Rope
Central Rock Gym

Great For indoor top rop climbing, for those who wish to test their endurance on the wall or a great starting point for those who are looking to eventually transition into outdoor cllimbing. This gym has a wide variety of top rope climbs, including auto-belays, lead climbing, and even some speed climbing setups! This is a great place for those of all skill levels and styles. From the set up of the gym it is clear they put incredible thought into their setting, ensuring that it is tailored for its gym community.

Outdoor Boulder
Joshua Tree National Park

This beautiful park in southern california is known for its iconic Joshua Trees and rock formations. These rocks are perfect for some outdoor bouldering, and theres a wide range of boulders to start off on if it's your first time bouldering. I particulary like that you can actually rent crash pads, so this is a perfect spot for first time outdoor boulderers who may not have the proper equipment or are unsure of whether they want to invest in that just yet.

Outdoor Top Rope
Yosemite National Park

Known for it's amazing views and iconic climbs. Many know this national park as the home to the granite giant El Capitan, the site of some of climbing's greatest achivements, but there are plenty of top rope sites more inviting to beginners. If you're not ready for top rope yet, the hikes here are challenging and many include some scrambles which give off the feel of rock climbing without being roped in. Though, Yosemite is often full of expert climbers who are usually open to guiding beginners.
